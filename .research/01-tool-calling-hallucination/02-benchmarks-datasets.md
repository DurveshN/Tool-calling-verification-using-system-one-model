## 2. Reliability warning for evaluation design
"Benchmarking the Benchmarks" (https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02577) audited BFCL v4, tau2-Bench, LiveMCPBench, MCP-Atlas: 18.5% evaluator-human disagreement on 496 tasks (92 disagreements); LiveMCPBench repeated runs ranged 57.9% to 76.8%. Relevant if the user's paper reports small deltas.

## 3. Best fits for a per-call verifier (see also 05 file)
1. FC-RewardBench (paired correct/incorrect, single-turn, BFCL-derived) - closest to "score one call".
2. ToolPRMBench and AgentProcessBench - step-level labels in multi-step trajectories (needed for "call + result" scoring).
3. BFCL v3/v4 + tau2-bench - end-to-end downstream gain measurement (as in Reinforced Agent).
4. ToolBH / SimpleToolHalluBench / HTB - hallucination-by-construction negatives (no-tool, fake-tool).
5. ToolFailBench / Fabrication-After-Tool-Failure - result-side errors, which a "call + result" verifier is positioned to catch.
